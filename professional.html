PROFESSIONAL EDUCATION

Empowering Dental Professionals
Through Education

Dental professional education is a comprehensive, multi-stage journey that begins with a rigorous undergraduate degree, such as a BDS or DDS/DMD, focusing on foundational sciences and supervised clinical practice. Following graduation, many practitioners pursue postgraduate specialization in areas like orthodontics or oral surgery to master specific dental disciplines. This learning process continues throughout a career via mandatory Continuing Dental Education (CDE), which ensures dentists remain proficient in evolving technologies like digital imaging and laser treatments while maintaining their professional licensure.
