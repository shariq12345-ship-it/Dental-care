OUR PRODUCTS

Toothpaste

Dental toothpaste serves as a crucial component of oral hygiene, utilizing active ingredients like fluoride, xylitol, or desensitizing agents to remove plaque, strengthen enamel, and prevent cavities. Popular brands such as Colgate, Sensodyne, and Medicam provide options for whitening, sensitivity, and herbal care, with pastes often categorized by their specific dental benefits.

  • Whitening Toothpaste
  • Sensitive Teeth Toothpaste
  • Fluoride Strengthening
  • Abrasives Cleaning

Brushes

Dental brushes are specialized tools used to clean the surfaces of teeth and the gaps between them that regular brushing might miss. For a complete oral hygiene routine, dental professionals recommend using a combination of a standard toothbrush and interdental cleaning tools.

  • Plaque: Removes sticky bacterial film from tooth surfaces.
  • Gaps: Specifically designed to clean the spaces between teeth.
  • Gums: Massages tissue and helps prevent inflammation and disease.
  • Reach: Accesses hard-to-reach areas that standard brushes miss.

Special products
for kids

Specialized dental products for kids focus on encouraging healthy habits through fun, child-friendly designs and safe ingredients. Top recommendations include electric toothbrushes featuring popular characters (Oral-B Kids, Philips Sonicare), flavored, fluoride-free toothpaste (Boka, Hello), and educational dentist role-play sets that help make oral care exciting.

  • Sugar-Free Gum: Brands like PUR and Project 7 are great for freshening breath and are braces-safe.
  • Dentist Gags/Toys: Crocodile mouth bite games and other dental-themed toys.
  • Educational Dentist Kits: Simulation kits like those from Bachaa Party allow kids to play dentist and learn about teeth care.

Electrical Accessories

Essential dental electrical accessories include LED curing lights, electric micromotors/handpieces, air/water solenoid valves, and dental foot pedals, which power and control handpieces. Key components for dental chairs include LED operating lights, sensor controls, and dental chair motors, along with components like autoclave machines for sterilization and dental x-ray units.

  • Motors & Handpieces: Dental Brushless LED Micromotors, E-type micro motors, and high-speed handpiece accessories.
  • Unit Control & Parts: Foot paddle controls, dental solenoid valves, pneumatic valves, and microswitches for unit operation.
  • Illumination & Visibility: LED curing lights, viewer lamps, and intraoral lamps.
  • Operating Components: Turbine bottles, dental compressors, and dental chair motor controllers.
