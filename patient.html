PATIENT EDUCATION

Patient Education for
Oral & Dental Health

Patient Education for Oral & Dental Health is an essential part of dental care that focuses on providing patients with general and necessary information to maintain good oral hygiene and prevent dental diseases. It helps individuals understand the importance of proper brushing and flossing techniques, healthy dietary habits, and regular dental check-ups in preserving oral health. Patient education also guides patients about common dental problems such as tooth decay, gum disease, and bad breath, along with their prevention and early management. By increasing awareness and encouraging self-care practices, it plays a key role in reducing the risk of oral infections, improving overall health, and promoting lifelong healthy teeth and gums.

Common Dental Problems

  • Gum disease (gingivitis/periodontitis) – swollen, bleeding gums.
  • Tooth decay (cavities) – caused by plaque and sugar.
  • Tooth sensitivity – pain with hot/cold foods.
  • Bad breath (halitosis) – often due to poor hygiene or infection.
  • Oral infections/ulcers – may need professional care.

Daily Oral Hygiene Practices

  • Brush teeth twice daily (morning & before bed) with fluoride toothpaste.
  • Use a soft-bristled toothbrush.
  • Replace toothbrush every 3 months.
  • Clean between teeth using floss or interdental brushes.
  • Rinse with an antibacterial mouthwash if recommended.
  • Brush for at least 2 minutes each time.
